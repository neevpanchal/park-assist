Parking rules

How campus parking works

The essentials are below. The full rules follow.

7 AM – 9 PM

Open daily. Book 1 to 14 hours in a row.

15 minutes

Arrive and show your QR pass at the gate within 15 minutes of your start time.

3 strikes

Each missed arrival is a strike. 3 strikes pauses booking and bans your vehicle.

₹50 / 30 min

Fine for staying past your booked end time, paid at the exit gate.

1

General Parking Facility Regulations

01

Account Verification

Students must maintain a verified account with valid university email and registered vehicle plate details to book bays.

02

Official Bookings Only

All reservations must be completed in advance through this official web portal. On-spot unofficial parking is strictly prohibited.

03

Operating Schedule

Facility operates from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily. Vehicles may not be parked outside of operating hours.

04

Multi-Hour Bookings

Reservations may span from 1 to 14 consecutive hours. Each reservation has an explicit start time and ending deadline.

05

15-Minute Grace Period

Patrons must arrive and complete arrival check-in within 15 minutes of their scheduled reservation start time.

06

No-Show Strike System

Failing to check in within 15 minutes automatically marks the booking as a No-Show, issues 1 strike, and releases the space.

07

Three-Strike Suspension

Accumulating 3 strikes automatically suspends the student's booking privileges and bans the registered vehicle from the facility.

08

Assigned Bay Parking

Park strictly within your designated bay (A01 to A50). Parking across multiple bays or lanes is subject to towing.

09

Non-Transferable Rights

Parking permits are non-transferable and tied exclusively to the student identity and registered vehicle plate number.

10

Security Instructions

All patrons must comply with directions and speed controls issued by university parking security personnel and barrier operators.

11

Advance Cancellations

If you no longer require a parking space, cancel your booking in advance on the portal to release the slot for other students.

12

Prohibited Areas

Never block perimeter aisles, corner exits (Gates A, B, C, D), pedestrian walkways, or designated electric vehicle/security stalls.

2

Official Vehicle Overstay Policy

A student must vacate their assigned bay and exit the facility before their scheduled reservation window concludes. For example, if you book a bay from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, your vehicle must exit through one of the four gates by 12:00 PM.

STEP 1

Gate Exit Logging

On-duty gate security logs actual departure timestamps when vehicles cross the barrier gate.

STEP 2

Penalty Assessment

Overstays beyond a 5-minute buffer are assessed at ₹50 per 30 minutes of excess occupancy.

STEP 3

Offline Fee Settlement

Security officers enforce penalty collection and verify payment clearance before opening barrier gates.

STEP 4

Disciplinary Record

Overstay incidents are recorded in your permanent violation log separately from no-show attendance strikes.
